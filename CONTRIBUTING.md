# Helping MuslimSim

Thank you for taking an interest in improving MuslimSim. The immediate focus is reliability, usability, and hardware compatibility. There is no paid launch announced; a possible future paid release is intended to use a one-time purchase model rather than a subscription.

## Start with an issue

Bug reports, reproduction steps, hardware compatibility findings, usability feedback, and development proposals are welcome. Describe what you expected, what happened, and the simulator, aircraft, hardware model, and project revision involved. Keep logs short and remove credentials, personal information, and device serial numbers.

## Before contributing code, artwork, or documentation

Open an issue describing the work before investing substantial time or submitting a patch. The maintainer and contributor must agree in writing on the applicable contribution and licensing terms before that material is accepted or merged, including whether it may be used in a future paid release. A general contribution agreement is not yet in place.

Submitting an issue or pull request does not by itself transfer copyright to the maintainer. No payment, revenue share, ownership stake, free commercial license, or other reward is promised unless separately agreed in writing. Contributors should not assume that future MuslimSim releases will remain free or that their code can later be relicensed without the necessary permission.

This document is a contribution process notice, not a copyright assignment or a substitute for an agreed license. The maintainer should leave code contributions unmerged until the terms are resolved. Existing third-party licenses continue to apply to their covered material; this process does not remove those rights.

## Working on an agreed change

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), keep changes focused, and preserve working device behavior and saved assignments. Describe the problem, the change, the checks performed, and any remaining live hardware checks. Do not add credentials, private captures, or third-party material without appropriate permission and attribution.

See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) for the current licensing position.
